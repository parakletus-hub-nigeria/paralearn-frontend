Decoupling CBT Engine into an Autonomous Microservice
To: Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
Target: cbt.pln.ng Architecture
Date: September 2026
Status: Architectural Proposal

Executive Summary

Following an architectural evaluation of the ParaLearn platform (NestJS backend & Next.js frontend), we recommend extracting the Computer-Based Testing (CBT) engine as an autonomous microservice (cbt.pln.ng). This move resolves critical high-concurrency exam spikes that currently threaten core school operations, while unlocking a high-growth self-serve market for tutorial centres (JAMB/WAEC prep), independent tutors, and testing institutes without requiring full School Information System (SIS) onboarding.

1. Strategic & Operational Rationale

  • Resource & Traffic Asymmetry: During exam weeks, thousands of students simultaneously hit servers with start requests, answer updates, 20-second proctoring heartbeats, and auto-submits. Decoupling isolates connection pools and ensures report card generation, bursary billing, and admin tasks run unaffected.
  • Product-Led Growth (PLG) & TAM Expansion: Full school onboarding requires academic sessions, terms, classes, subject teachers, and grading scales—a major barrier for standalone examiners. Decoupling allows tutors to register in 30 seconds, create a test, and distribute PINs.
  • Dual-Access Synergy: Existing ParaLearn schools retain Single Sign-On (SSO) and automatic score-sheet sync, while independent examiners get a lean, self-serve testing hall.

3. Core Architectural Concept: The "Workspace" Abstraction

To cleanly support both ParaLearn institutional schools and independent tutors without maintaining duplicate codebases, the CBT service introduces an abstract Workspace model:

cbt.pln.ng │ ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐ ▼ ▼ [Mode A: ParaLearn School] [Mode B: Standalone Exam Hall] • Auth: SSO via ParaLearn JWT • Auth: Self-Serve (Email / Google) • Workspace: School Tenant • Workspace: Tutorial Centre / Tutor • Roster: Synced from School Classes • Roster: Ad-hoc PINs / Public Link • Egress: Auto-feeds Term Report Cards • Egress: Instant Results / CSV Export
Mode A: ParaLearn School User (SSO)

School admin or teacher logs in via ParaLearn credentials. The CBT backend verifies the JWT against the shared signing authority and maps the session to an INSTITUTION workspace with existing classes and subjects preloaded.

Mode B: Standalone Exam Hall (Self-Serve)

Independent tutors sign up directly with Email or Google OAuth. Creates a STANDALONE_HALL workspace. Zero SIS setup required—tutors immediately create question banks and launch tests.

4. Frictionless Candidate Delivery: Roster vs. Exam PIN

Tutorial centres and walk-in lesson centers cannot manage permanent student accounts. The CBT microservice supports two distinct delivery strategies:

  • Roster Model (Schools): Permanent student account (student ID + password). Results attach to academic transcripts and end-of-term report cards.
  • Exam PIN & Direct Link Model (Tutors & Tutorial Centres): Tutors share a direct link (e.g. cbt.pln.ng/take/JAMB-MOCK-26). Candidates enter their Full Name + One-Time Access PIN and take the test immediately without account friction.

6. High-Throughput Infrastructure & Data Sync Contract

  • Redis In-Memory State:
    • Active Timers & Locks: Prevents multi-device logins and synchronizes countdowns accurate to milliseconds.
    • Ephemeral Answer Buffer: Caches in-progress candidate keystrokes and answers in Redis, eliminating thousands of database writes per minute. Answers are bulk-flushed to PostgreSQL only on final submission.
  • Inter-Service Communication:
    • Ingress: Read-only gRPC or internal REST endpoints to pull class rosters from ParaLearn Core for school exams.
    • Egress (Score Sync): When an institutional exam concludes, the CBT service emits an exam.completed event to BullMQ/Redis. ParaLearn Core consumes this and executes an atomic POST /scores/batch-save to update report cards automatically.
